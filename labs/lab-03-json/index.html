About the Event

SEU Tech Fest 2026 is a university-wide technology event organised by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southeast University (SEU). The event brings together students from every department to explore programming, robotics, web development, and emerging technologies through friendly competitions, workshops, and exhibitions.

Goals of the Event

The main goal of SEU Tech Fest is to encourage students to apply classroom knowledge to real-world problems. The event also aims to build teamwork, improve problem-solving skills, and create a platform where students can showcase their projects to faculty members, industry professionals, and peers.

Who Can Participate

The event is open to all currently enrolled SEU students, regardless of department or semester. Participants can join individually for the programming contest or form teams of up to four members for the project showcase and hackathon segments.

Event Schedule & Participation Details

The event runs for a full day and is divided into several segments so that students with different interests can all find something useful to join. Details of each segment and the steps required to participate are given below.

Event Segments

  • Competitive Programming Contest
  • Web Development Hackathon
  • Robotics and IoT Exhibition
  • Guest Seminar on Artificial Intelligence

Steps to Participate

  1. Read the event rules published on the SEU CSE department notice board.
  2. Prepare your entry before the deadline:
    • Choose your preferred segment (contest, hackathon, or exhibition).
    • Form a team if the segment requires one.
    • Prepare any required project files or ID documents.
  3. Fill in the registration form on this page and submit it.
  4. Collect your confirmation email and event ID card on the event day.
